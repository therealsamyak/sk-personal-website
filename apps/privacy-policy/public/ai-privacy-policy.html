GoGCLI · Executor

AI integration privacy policy

GoGCLI connects your Google account to a self-hosted Executor instance for tasks you request through your chosen AI agents.

Effective November 11, 2026

Google data and permissions

The integration accesses Google data only through the permissions you authorize. Access is read-only for Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Contacts, Tasks, Meet, and supported Photos data.

Calendar access includes reading and changing calendars and events when you request those actions. Other connected services cannot change your Google data through this integration.

How your data is used

Executor uses Google data to answer your questions and perform your requested tasks. Tool results can include messages, documents, contact details, and calendar information.

Results may be sent to the AI agents and model providers you choose. Their handling and retention follow their policies and your account settings. Review those settings before requesting sensitive information.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. The integration does not use Google data to develop or train generalized AI models. Access, use, and transfer follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Credentials, storage, and removal

OAuth credentials are encrypted in the self-hosted Executor deployment. Authorized administrators control the deployment, its storage, and its backups. Encryption does not prevent access by an authorized administrator.

Disconnect your account in Executor to remove its stored credentials and connection. You can also revoke Google access at any time.

Revocation stops future authorized access. It does not erase results already sent to an agent or provider. Logs, cached results, and backups can have separate retention periods. Use the deployment and provider controls to manage those copies.

This website and analytics

This public page uses self-hosted Umami for aggregate page statistics, such as page views, referring sites, browser types, and approximate locations. Tracking excludes URL queries and fragments and honors your browser’s Do Not Track setting.

No Google account data or credentials are sent to Umami. This page uses no advertising cookies, session recording, forms, or embedded media. Cloudflare delivers the page and processes request metadata for delivery and security.

Questions and changes

For access or deletion requests, contact the administrator through the channel supplied when you received access. Changes to this policy appear here with an updated effective date.
